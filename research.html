IMD Historical Meteorological Data

Long-term temperature observations structured for spatial intelligence models

Historical grid data (GRD format) provided by the India Meteorological Department (IMD), Pune, covers regional daily maximum temperatures across India. These spatial-temporal matrices are interpolated and pre-processed to construct historical base baselines, identify heat anomaly trends, and train neural network weights.

AI Spatio-Temporal Prediction Models

Core deep learning and regression architectures powering heatwave forecasts

ConvLSTM Network

Acc: 94.2%

Captures spatial heat propagation and temporal shifts concurrently, projecting heatwave evolution across regions.

Layers: 4 Conv + 2 LSTM Loss Function: Mean Squared Error

XGBoost Regressor

Acc: 91.8%

Calculates extreme point temperature thresholds using pressure anomalies and historical averages.

Estimators: 500 decision trees Depth: Max depth 8

FFN Classifier

Acc: 96.5%

Categorizes risk alerts (Extreme / Severe / Mild / Normal) by combining humidity thresholds with peak forecasts.

Inputs: Temp, Anomaly, Relative Humidity Outputs: 4 Alert Classes

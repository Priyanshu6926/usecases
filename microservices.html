Decoupling Advisory Generation into an Autonomous Microservice

Under the Somaiya-IMD AI Framework, advisory generation experiences sudden burst loads when emergency heatwave warnings are declared. Extracting Phase 5 into an independent FastAPI RESTful Microservice (Port 8001) enables elastic horizontal scaling, Pydantic type validation, and zero single points of failure.

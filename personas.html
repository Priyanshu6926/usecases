Lab Viva Question: Explain the Impact of AI on Social Media

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has fundamentally restructured social media ecosystems from passive broadcasting feeds into hyper-personalized, predictive interaction engines. The primary dimensions of AI impact include:

  1. Algorithmic Feed Personalization & Ranking: Deep recommendation networks (e.g., TikTok's Monolith, Meta's DLRM) process watch time, taps, and social graphs in real time to curate endless feeds maximizing engagement and dwell time.
  2. Predictive Ad Targeting & Bidding: Machine learning algorithms predict conversion probabilities ($pCTR$, $pCVR$) to serve dynamic product ads to precisely segmented buyer personas at optimal bid valuations.
  3. Generative AI Content Creation: Automated generation of copy, responsive creative variants, localized video captions, and visual assets at scale.
  4. Social Listening & Real-Time Sentiment Analysis: NLP models ingest brand mentions across millions of daily posts to identify emerging PR crises, sentiment polarity, and viral brand affinity.
  5. Conversational Support Chatbots: Automated LLM agents resolve inquiries in direct messaging channels 24/7, reducing response times from hours to milliseconds.

Conclusion: AI transforms customer data into granular persona intelligence, enabling organizations to eliminate marketing waste, lower customer acquisition costs (CAC), and maximize customer lifetime value (LTV).

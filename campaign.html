Write note on: Google AdSense (Monetization framework for web publishers vs Google AdWords/Ads).

Technical Summary of Google AdSense

Google AdSense is an advertising program launched by Google that allows webmasters and website owners (publishers) to monetize their online content by displaying targeted Google advertisements (text, display images, responsive banners, native video ads) on their web pages.

  • AdWords vs AdSense: AdWords (Google Ads) is for advertisers paying to buy traffic, while AdSense is for publishers earning money by displaying those ads.
  • Revenue Share: Google passes 68% of the revenue to the publisher for AdSense for Content, and 51% for AdSense for Search.
  • Monetization Models: Publishers earn via CPC (Cost Per Click) and CPM / vCPM (Cost Per Thousand Active Viewable Impressions).
  • Ad Delivery Mechanism: Automatically matches ads based on page keywords, user browsing cookies, and demographic context via an instant programmatic auction.

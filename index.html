The Challenge

Understanding heat vulnerability

Heatwaves are among the most lethal extreme weather events, increasing in frequency, intensity, and duration due to global warming and rapid urban micro-climate changes. Prolonged heat exposure severely degrades public health, drains agricultural productivity, stresses municipal grids, and threatens livelihoods.

Because heatwave onset and severity vary highly across regions due to local topography and urban heat islands, generic forecasts fall short. Precise prediction requires integrating macro-scale meteorological data with ground-level real-time sensing.

The Architecture

Integration of AI models & IoT sensing

1

Data Acquisition Layer Data

Acquiring long-term IMD historical meteorological datasets (GRD) alongside real-time feeds from deployed AWS grids.

2

Analytics & Intelligence Layer AI Models

Executing spatio-temporal predictions, forecasting maximum temperatures, and classifying severity grades.

3

Validation & Decision Support UI & LLM

Validating predictions using local AWS readings and deploying LLM models to generate context-specific advisories.

Target Beneficiaries

Empowering stakeholders with actionable climate intelligence

Disaster Authorities

Providing State & Municipal Disaster Management (SDMA) with hotspot warnings to allocate resources and open cooling centers.

Agricultural Agencies

Distributing localized predictions to protect livestock, adjust irrigation cycles, and safeguard field laborers.

Public Health & Citizens

Alerting hospitals of impending heatstroke surges and warning high-risk demographics with direct advisories.
